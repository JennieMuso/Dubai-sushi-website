Jin Xiwei

Precision, with something personal.

Jin Xiwei’s cooking begins with respect for the ingredient and a belief that technique should create clarity. His point of view brings Chinese sensibility into conversation with Japanese craft.

At JIN, that language becomes more intimate. Every course is prepared in view, paced for the room, and shaped around the guest.

Approach
Ingredient-led cooking, disciplined technique, and a restrained style that leaves space for flavour.
Perspective
A dialogue between Chinese culinary memory and the precision of Japanese omakase.
At JIN
A counter experience where craft is visible and every guest feels part of the exchange.

A career shaped by curiosity.

Jin’s culinary language has been formed by years of exacting kitchen discipline and a continuous search for balance. Rather than treating tradition as fixed, he studies its principles, then applies them with confidence and restraint.

The result is food that feels composed but alive: precise in its execution, generous in its intention, and memorable in ways that reveal themselves after the meal.

The counter becomes the conversation.

“Technique matters most when it allows the ingredient to speak clearly.”

Jin Xiwei · concept statement

His signature is restraint.

Nothing is added without purpose. Acidity brings light, heat arrives with control, and texture is used to extend the character of each ingredient.

The menu at JIN is imagined as the clearest expression of that philosophy: one course leading naturally to the next, with surprise held inside a calm structure.
